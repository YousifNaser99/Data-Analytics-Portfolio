## 📌 Business Objective
Analyze supermarket sales performance and customer behavior to uncover insights
into revenue distribution, sales trends, and customer demographics,
supporting data-driven retail decision-making.

## 📊 Data Source
- Public Kaggle dataset: Supermarket Sales – Dataset (Myanmar)
- Includes branch, city, product line, payment method, gender, customer type,
  quantity, total amount, tax, and date of purchase

## 🔍 Analytical Process
- **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, standardized column names,
  and formatted dates for time-series analysis using Excel.
- **Data Modeling:** Built relationships between categorical dimensions
  (city, branch, product line, gender, payment method) and numerical metrics.
- **Dashboard Design:** Developed a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📄 Dashboard Pages
- **Sales Overview:** KPIs for total sales, tax, and average unit price with filters.
- **Customer Insights:** Segmentation by gender, customer type, and payment method.
- **Monthly & Regional Analysis:** Sales distribution by month, city, and branch.
- **Target Tracking:** Comparison of actual sales against predefined goals.

## 📈 Key Insights
- Identified top-performing branches and cities.
- Revealed customer purchasing patterns and payment preferences.
- Highlighted underperforming product lines for strategic action.
