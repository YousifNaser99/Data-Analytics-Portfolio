### Consolidating 7 Dashboards into a Single Executive Report

## 📌 Business Problem
Hospital senior management relied on seven disconnected dashboards across Operations,
HR, Finance, Pharmacy, and Inventory, resulting in fragmented insights and delayed
strategic decision-making.

**Key Challenge:** Transform scattered operational and financial data into a unified,
executive-ready report that enables clear, data-driven decisions.

**Before:** 7 disconnected dashboards  
**After:** 1 consolidated executive report

## 🎯 Project Objective
Consolidate insights from multiple hospital dashboards into a single comprehensive
executive report that provides a holistic view of hospital performance
and supports strategic leadership decisions.

## 📊 Data Sources
Data extracted from seven hospital dashboards, including:
- **Operational Data:** Patient volumes, annual visits, diagnosis distribution
- **HR Data:** Salary cost, average salary, cost by specialty
- **Financial Data:** Treatment cost, revenue per diagnosis, repeat visit rate
- **Pharmacy & Inventory:** Stock quantities, values, and prescription trends

## 🔍 Analytical Approach
### Data Preparation & Modeling
- Cleaned, transformed, and unified metrics from all dashboards
- Built a multi-dimensional model linking operations, finance, HR, and inventory

### KPI Framework
KPIs structured across four strategic pillars:
- **Operational Performance:** Visit rate stability, top diagnoses
- **Human Cost:** Total salary cost, highest-cost specialties
- **Revenue:** Revenue drivers and repeat visit rate
- **Inventory:** Inventory value and category concentration

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Operational Performance:** Visit rate volatility dropped from 101 to 69,
  highlighting the need for growth stabilization initiatives.
- **Human Cost Efficiency:** Surgeon salaries represented the highest cost segment ($1.30M),
  indicating the need for workload-cost alignment.
- **Revenue Drivers:** Cancer and Asthma ranked highest in both visit volume and revenue.
- **Inventory Risk:** Inventory value reached $7.68M, with Antiviral stock accounting
  for 25% of total quantity, signaling concentration risk.

## 🧠 Executive Recommendations
- Prioritize investment in high-revenue specialties (Cancer & Asthma)
- Align surgeon compensation with operational workload
- Implement initiatives to stabilize and grow patient visits
- Review inventory storage and stock policies to reduce overstocking risk

## 📈 Business Impact
- Replaced fragmented dashboards with a unified executive view
- Improved HR cost efficiency through data-driven insights
- Reduced inventory risk and optimized stock management
- Aligned operational actions with financial outcomes
