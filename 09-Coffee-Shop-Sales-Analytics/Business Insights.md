# Business Insights

This project provides actionable business insights derived from six months of coffee shop sales data.

---

# Executive Insights

- Total Revenue reached approximately **$699K** across all stores.
- Three stores contributed to the overall business performance with noticeable differences in revenue and customer purchasing behavior.
- Average Order Value (AOV) varied by store, highlighting opportunities to improve pricing and product mix.

---

# Product Insights

- A small number of products generated the majority of total revenue following the Pareto (80/20) principle.
- ABC Classification identified high-value products requiring inventory and sales prioritization.
- Several low-performing products contributed little to overall revenue and may require promotional strategies or discontinuation.

---

# Store Performance Insights

- Hell's Kitchen generated the highest total revenue.
- Lower Manhattan achieved the highest Average Order Value.
- Store performance differences indicate varying customer purchasing behaviors rather than only transaction volume.

---

# Time Analysis Insights

- Sales activity showed clear peak hours during the day.
- Revenue trends changed across months, allowing comparison of month-over-month growth.
- Heatmap analysis revealed the busiest sales periods by day and hour.

---

# Operational Insights

The automated ETL pipeline improved data reliability through:

- Automated validation
- Data cleaning
- Business rule verification
- SQL audit logging
- Human approval workflow
- Production deployment

This ensured that Power BI reports consumed only validated and approved datasets.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on Class A products.
- Investigate pricing strategies used by the highest AOV store.
- Review low-performing products for optimization.
- Increase staffing during peak sales hours.
- Continue monitoring monthly sales trends for proactive business decisions.
- Maintain automated data quality checks before reporting.

---

# Conclusion

This solution demonstrates an end-to-end Business Intelligence pipeline integrating Python, n8n, SQL Server, and Power BI to transform raw sales data into reliable business insights that support data-driven decision-making.
