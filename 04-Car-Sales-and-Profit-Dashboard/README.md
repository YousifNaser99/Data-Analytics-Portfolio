## 📌 Business Objective
Develop a comprehensive and interactive sales dashboard to analyze car sales data,
with a focus on sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and inventory management.
The dashboard supports data-driven decisions to optimize sales strategies,
improve profitability, and manage stock effectively.

## 📊 Data Source
- Transactional database containing historical car sales records
- Includes customer demographics (country, town)
- Car details (make, model, color)
- Sales date, sales price, and profit margins

## 🔍 Analytical Process
- **Data Modeling:** Cleaned, transformed, and structured raw data into a relational model.
- **KPI Definition:** Defined key KPIs including Total Sales, Total Profit, Net Profit, and Profit Margin.
- **Dashboard Design:** Built an intuitive Power BI dashboard with interactive slicers
  for Country, Town, Make, Model, and Sale Date.
- **Data Visualization:** Created multiple charts to analyze sales trends, customer segmentation,
  and inventory performance.
- **Sales & Profit Analysis:** Monitored overall financial performance and sales trends over time.
- **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Analyzed customer distribution and purchasing behavior.
- **Stock & Inventory Analysis:** Tracked stock cost, average cost, and product breakdowns.

## 📈 Key Insights
- Identified top-performing products and regions.
- Improved visibility of profitability and margin performance.
- Enabled data-driven inventory management and targeted sales strategies.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX, Interactive Dashboards)
- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning and Preparation)
