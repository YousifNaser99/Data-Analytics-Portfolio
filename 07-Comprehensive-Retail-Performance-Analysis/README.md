## 📌 Business Problem
Retail management relied on raw transactional sales data and basic reports without
a unified analytical view, resulting in limited visibility into profit drivers,
loss-making products, and operational inefficiencies.

Insights were scattered across tables and isolated metrics, slowing strategic
decision-making and limiting the ability to optimize pricing, discounts,
and product performance.

**Key Challenge:** Transform raw retail sales data into a single executive-ready
dashboard that clearly explains what drives profit, what causes losses,
and where strategic actions are required.

**Before:** Raw transactional data with no strategic structure  
**After:** One consolidated executive profitability dashboard

## 🎯 Project Objective
Analyze sales and profitability performance and transform raw retail data into
a comprehensive executive dashboard that supports data-driven decisions across
products, customers, regions, pricing, and operations.

## 📊 Data Source
Public retail dataset (Kaggle) containing:
- **Sales Data:** Orders, revenue, quantity, discounts
- **Customer Data:** Segments and purchase behavior
- **Product Data:** Categories, sub-categories, product-level performance
- **Geographic Data:** Regional sales and profitability
- **Operational Data:** Shipping modes and delivery time

## 🔍 Analytical Approach
### Data Preparation & Modeling
- Cleaned and transformed raw Excel data
- Built analytical SQL Views to handle business logic and calculated metrics
- Designed a performance-optimized Power BI data model
- Developed DAX measures for KPIs and advanced profitability analysis

### KPI Framework
KPIs structured across five strategic pillars:
- **Sales Performance:** Revenue, order trends, average order value
- **Profitability:** Total profit, profit margin, loss-making products
- **Customer Performance:** Revenue and profit by customer segment
- **Pricing & Discounts:** Discount impact on profit margins
- **Operations & Logistics:** Shipping efficiency and delivery impact on profit

## 📈 Key Insights
- High sales volume does not always translate into high profitability
- Technology was the strongest profit contributor, while Furniture showed margin pressure
- Copiers and Phones generated strong profit, while Tables and Furnishings showed losses
- Consumer segment drove revenue, while Corporate showed higher average order value
- Discount-driven growth increased volume but reduced margins
- Shipping decisions had a direct impact on profitability

## 🧠 Executive Recommendations
- Optimize discount policies by category and sub-category
- Focus on high-margin products and re-evaluate loss-making items
- Apply differentiated pricing strategies across customer segments
- Improve shipping decisions for low-margin orders
- Prioritize regions and products with the highest profit potential

## 📈 Business Impact
- Clear identification of profit drivers and loss areas
- Improved pricing and discount strategies
- Optimized product and customer focus
- Alignment of operational decisions with financial outcomes
- Replacement of static reporting with interactive executive dashboards
