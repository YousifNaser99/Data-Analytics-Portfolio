# Key Business Insights

## Reliability & Downtime Insights

- Wear and corrosion were identified as major contributors to equipment downtime, indicating opportunities for preventive maintenance improvement.
- A limited number of failure modes contributed significantly to total downtime hours and production impact.
- High-criticality equipment requires continuous monitoring to reduce operational risks.

---

## Maintenance Cost Insights

- Parts cost represented the largest component of maintenance expenditure compared with labor and other maintenance costs.
- Cost analysis highlighted equipment and failure categories with the highest financial impact.
- Optimizing spare parts planning can help reduce unnecessary maintenance spending.

---

## Inventory Management Insights

- Inventory analysis identified high-value spare parts and items requiring better stock optimization.
- Parts below reorder level should be monitored to avoid maintenance delays and production interruptions.
- Balancing inventory availability with inventory cost can improve operational efficiency.

---

## Equipment Performance Insights

- Some equipment assets generated a disproportionate share of downtime and maintenance costs.
- Critical assets should be prioritized for reliability improvement programs.
- Failure history can support predictive maintenance and asset management strategies.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Implement preventive maintenance strategies based on failure patterns.
- Prioritize high-impact equipment for reliability improvement initiatives.
- Optimize spare parts inventory using consumption and criticality analysis.
- Monitor downtime trends regularly to identify emerging operational risks.
