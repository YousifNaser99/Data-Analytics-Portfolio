# Business Insights

Business insights extracted from the Sales Analytics Platform to support data-driven decision making.

---

# Executive Overview

The platform provides a complete business performance analysis covering:

- Revenue performance
- Profitability analysis
- Customer behavior
- Product performance
- Market basket relationships
- Predictive analytics

---

# Revenue & Profitability Insights

## Overall Performance

Key metrics analyzed:

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Units Sold

The analysis provides visibility into overall business growth and profitability.

---

# Product Insights

## Product Performance

The analysis identifies:

- Best-performing products
- Revenue contribution by category
- Product ranking
- ABC analysis classification

Pareto analysis helps identify products that contribute the highest percentage of revenue.

---

# Customer Insights

Customer analytics focuses on:

- Customer segmentation
- Customer lifetime value
- Repeat customer behavior
- Customer purchasing patterns

These insights support customer retention and marketing strategies.

---

# Market Basket Analysis

Product relationships were analyzed using:

- Support
- Confidence
- Lift

The analysis helps identify products frequently purchased together and supports cross-selling opportunities.
