# Dashboard Overview

Interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, operational efficiency, and product insights.

The dashboard transforms cleaned and modeled data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI monitoring, advanced analytics, and decision-support reporting.

---

# Dashboard Pages

## Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of business performance through key KPIs including Revenue, Profit, Orders, Customers, Units Sold, and Profit Margin.

## Sales Performance
Analyzes sales trends, revenue distribution, product performance, country contribution, and sales growth patterns.

## Market Basket Analysis
Identifies product associations using Support, Confidence, and Lift metrics to discover purchasing relationships and recommendation opportunities.

## Customer Analytics 1
Analyzes customer segments, customer value, revenue contribution, and customer behavior patterns.

## Customer Analytics 2
Provides deeper customer insights including demographics, occupation, income segments, and customer distribution analysis.

## Financial Performance
Analyzes financial KPIs including Revenue, Cost, Tax, Freight, Gross Profit, and Profit Margin performance.

## Operations
Evaluates operational efficiency through manufacturing time, units sold, freight analysis, and regional operational performance.

## Time Intelligence 1
Analyzes sales performance over time using monthly trends, yearly comparison, and growth analysis.

## Time Intelligence 2
Provides advanced time-based insights including running revenue, MoM growth, YoY analysis, and seasonal trends.

## Product Performance
Analyzes product contribution using product ranking, ABC Classification, Pareto Analysis, and product profitability metrics.
